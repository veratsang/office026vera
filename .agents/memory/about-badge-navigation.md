---
name: About badge navigation
description: User-authored design constraint for the About archive cover.
---

The About cover's three physical ID badges are the category navigation. Each badge image, number, category name, and click behavior must remain together as one full-card link; do not separate the labels into another list. On desktop, show all three badges in one horizontal row with consistent size and spacing, below the full-width cover. Mobile may stack them vertically; do not use the two-plus-one layout. When removing the badge image's white backdrop, preserve the enclosed white card faces and remove only the edge-connected background.

**Why:** The user repeated this requirement after prior About layouts separated the badge art from links, placed the three badges in a two-plus-one arrangement, and the white source backdrop appeared as unwanted squares.

**How to apply:** Preserve the integrated badge-as-entry structure and desktop one-row arrangement when adjusting About page layout, labels, or responsive behavior. For image transparency, remove only white pixels connected to the source edges so white badge interiors remain intact.
